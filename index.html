Featured project

Tapzio — a business card that never runs out.

Tapzio is a smart NFC business card. Tap it with any phone and it instantly shares your contact info, social links, and website — no app, no paper, no reprints.

  • Tap & share instantly — contact info, social links and website in one tap.
  • Works with any phone — NFC-enabled smartphones read it instantly, no app needed.
  • Never runs out — no paper cards, no reprints, no “I ran out of cards”.
  • One card for everything — your whole digital identity, in your wallet.
